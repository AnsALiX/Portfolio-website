I am a Software Engineering undergraduate at COMSATS Lahore driven by the challenge of turning complex logic into seamless digital experiences.


My technical journey began with a rigorous science foundation during my matriculation, which naturally evolved into studying Computer Science at Government College University (GCU). Today, I am focused on bridging heavy backend architecture with clean, responsive front-end execution.


My core workflow revolves around React Native, C++, Java, and Python. I thrive on understanding the deep-level mechanics of algorithms and building fully functional application prototypes from the ground up rather than relying blindly on pre-packaged abstractions.


I recently engineered a fully functioning React Native application prototype, optimizing for cross-platform mobile performance and an intuitive user experience.


On the logic and systems side, I architected NexusSystem, a C++ social network engine built entirely on custom data structures directed graphs, tries, max-heaps, and hash tables to dynamically map user connections.


I also led the deployment of Kabaar to Kamaal, an AI-driven web app containerized with Docker and hosted on Google Cloud Run, and coded a custom console-based ASCII Space Invaders game purely in C++.


Outside the terminal, I train in calisthenics and football, and study the narratives and art direction of series like Black Clover, Attack on Titan and Monster.
